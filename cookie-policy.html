Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

This portfolio does not currently set first-party cookies, use analytics or advertising trackers, or embed third-party feeds, videos, or social widgets. Fonts and icons are served from this site's own files. Theme changes are temporary and are not saved in browser storage.

Cookies and similar technologies

No cookie banner is currently shown because this site does not use optional analytics, advertising, or preference-storage cookies. The contact form is sent to Formspree only after you submit it; Formspree may process technical request information and apply automated anti-spam controls under its own policies. This site does not control cookies or storage on a third-party provider's own website.

This repository contains a custom-domain configuration for GitHub Pages. GitHub may operate essential hosting infrastructure or security logging under its own terms. A cookie or storage technology introduced by the host, Formspree, or a future integration should be reviewed before publication.

When this may change

If analytics, advertising, embedded media, persistent preferences, a browser-side CAPTCHA, or other non-essential storage is added, this policy must be updated. Depending on visitor location and the technology used, a consent choice may need to be collected before it runs. Check the Formspree spam-protection settings; do not add a Google reCAPTCHA widget or other external script until that review and any required consent mechanism are in place.
